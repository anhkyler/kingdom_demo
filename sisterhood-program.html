Keep Elevating Yourself Sisterhood Program

Mental, emotional, social, and life-readiness support for women 18 and up, in the company of sisters.

A sisterhood is a social, ethical, and emotional pact between women.

It’s based on an understanding that together we are stronger than we are alone. Empowerment is only possible if we come together and treat each other as sisters, not opponents. It’s a relationship built on our value as a collective, determined to make true change in the world.
