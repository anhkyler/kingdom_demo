Her story

Iantheya “Kayla” Brown-Benson is a woman of strength, resilience, and faith. Her life has been shaped by deep adversity: losing her son at 25 weeks pregnant, surviving domestic violence, and becoming a mother at a young age.

Though painful, these challenges built her relationship with God, leading her to surrender and trust in Him fully. Her faith anchored her when she felt she might lose hope.

Today, she calls herself a walking testimony to the power of Jesus Christ.

Iantheya and her husband, Christopher, live in Columbia with their four children, a constant source of love and joy. Through Kingdom K.E.Y.S., her mission is to give women and girls tools for joy, hope, and strength, rooted in the belief that each day offers a chance to improve.

Her doctorate in Education, focused on School and Community Leadership, gives her a strong understanding of how to lead and serve a community. She empowers others, showing service and dedication in all she does.

Beyond her nonprofit work, she is the author of Nurtured by the Good Shepherd, a 12-month journal inspired by Psalm 23. It grew from her own search for God’s peace, and she hopes it inspires others in their faith.

When she isn’t leading or serving, Iantheya enjoys reading, traveling, and time with her family. She reminds everyone she meets that God created us to live purpose-driven lives, and to lift others up, one day at a time.
