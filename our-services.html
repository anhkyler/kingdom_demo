Community services

Five parts of a fulfilling life in the Kingdom of God, and how we serve each one. Everything we offer is free.

  • Developing a strong personal relationship with Jesus

    Keep Elevating Yourself Sisterhood Program

    Mental, emotional, social, and life-readiness support for women 18 and up. We hope to inspire and encourage each woman to become self-sustaining and to contribute to her family, community, and society.

    About the sisterhood program

  • Walking with our brothers

    Brother’s Keeper

    A Kingdom K.E.Y.S. initiative for young men and men, ages 15 and up: Brotherhood Circles, mentorship, mental health workshops, and help connecting to counselors and community services.

    About Brother’s Keeper

  • Sharing the Gospel actively

    Educational workshops

    We host and partner with local schools and community agencies to bring empowerment and awareness. We lead conflict resolution groups for young girls in schools, support teachers in the classroom, and partner with local nonprofits to connect women, teenagers, and children with advocacy, case management, and help finding employment.

  • Serving others

    Volunteering

    We serve our community by volunteering with agencies, schools, and local events that need support, stepping in with hands-on help where volunteers are scarce. Collaboration strengthens the whole community, and by partnering with us you join a network of people making a real difference.

    Volunteer with us

  • Living with love and compassion

    Give-A-Ways

    We give hygiene products, household goods, diapers, and clothing and shoes to women, teenagers, and young girls in need.

  • Putting God’s will above all else

    Free Bibles

    Free Bibles in regular or large print, in the King James Version or the New Living Translation. Meet us in the community to receive yours, or have it shipped to you.
