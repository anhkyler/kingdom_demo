KeepElevatingYourself,Sister

Kingdom K.E.Y.S. is a nonprofit sisterhood in Columbia, South Carolina. We support women and girls in every part of life: social, emotional, mental, and spiritual. Every service we offer is free.

Join the sisterhood Ask for help

Who we are

  • Our mission

    To give women of all ages facing adversity a sisterhood that offers free guidance, support, and community resources, so women and young girls can become who God predestined them to be.

  • Our vision

    A positive impact on the lives of women of all ages in our community, in a way that is authentic, personal, and practical.

No shame. No judgement. Whatever brought you here, we’re here to serve you.

Everyone’s situation is different. We will never judge you or put you under pressure. We’re here to listen, answer your questions, and help in any way we can.
