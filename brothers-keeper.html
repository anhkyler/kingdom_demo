Brother’s Keeper

“Am I my brother’s keeper?” — Genesis 4:9

Yes. We are. Brother’s Keeper is a safe space for young men and men, ages 15 and up, to talk honestly about mental health, find support, and get connected to the resources they need.

About Brother’s Keeper

Too many men carry their struggles in silence.

They’ve been taught to push through, keep it to themselves, and never let anyone see them hurting. Brother’s Keeper exists to change that.

Born from the mission of Kingdom K.E.Y.S. (Keep Elevating Yourself, Sister), Brother’s Keeper extends the same commitment to growth, healing, and faith-centered support to the men in our community. We believe asking for help is a sign of strength, and that no man should have to walk through hard seasons alone.

We are our brothers’ keepers. That means showing up, listening without judgment, and walking with each other toward wholeness in mind, body, and spirit.

Who we serve

Brother’s Keeper welcomes young men and men ages 15 and up, wherever they are in life: high school students figuring out who they are, college students and young professionals under pressure, fathers, husbands, and men in every season of life.

You don’t need to have it all figured out, and you don’t need to be in crisis to belong here. You just need to be willing to show up.

Participants ages 15–17 need a parent or guardian’s consent to join programs.

What we offer

  • Brotherhood Circles

    Small peer support groups where men can talk openly about stress, relationships, identity, grief, and life’s pressures, led by trained facilitators.

    Join a circle

  • Mentorship

    We pair younger men with mentors who have walked similar roads and can offer guidance, encouragement, and accountability.

    Become a mentor

  • Mental Health Workshops

    Interactive sessions on topics like managing stress and anger, recognizing depression and anxiety, healthy relationships, and building emotional strength.

    Sign up for a workshop

  • Resource Connection

    Help finding counselors, therapists, and community services, plus support taking the first step to reach out.

    Ask us for help finding support

  • Faith and Wellness

    Opportunities to grow spiritually alongside mental and emotional healing, for those who want that path.

Our safe-space promise

Every Brother’s Keeper space is built on these commitments:

  • No judgment. Come as you are. Your story is welcome here.
  • Confidentiality. What’s shared in the circle stays in the circle, except when someone’s safety is at risk.
  • Respect. We listen to understand, not to fix or criticize.
  • Real talk. Honesty is encouraged, and no topic is too heavy.
  • Support, not substitute. We walk alongside you, and we connect you to licensed professionals when you need more.
